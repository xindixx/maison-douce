notre histoire

Made for the girl who romanticises her evenings

Maison Douce began with a simple wish: pajamas that feel like a soft exhale at the end of the day — and look pretty enough to keep on for a slow morning coffee.

Each piece is inspired by vintage French lingerie: scooped necklines, satin ribbon bows and floaty hems, cut generously so comfort always comes first.
