La Maison

For the woman who
dresses for herself

Maison Douce began with a quiet wish: pyjamas that feel like a soft exhale at the end of the day, and look lovely enough to keep on for a slow morning coffee.

Each piece borrows from vintage French lingerie: piped collars, satin ribbon ties, picot trims and floating hems. Everything is made in small batches and cut generously, so comfort always comes first.

I

Satin

Fluid and cool against the skin, with contrast piping and hand-placed crystals.

II

Pointelle

A soft, breathable knit with tiny open-work holes, edged in picot trim.

III

Chiffon

Sheer and airy, double-layered at the neckline for coverage. Hand wash cold, lay flat to dry.
